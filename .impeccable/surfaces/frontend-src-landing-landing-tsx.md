Mode: Persuade. A separate page at "/" that leads into the app at "/app".

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your documents stay inside the walls; only placeholders ride out to an AI provider. One continuous scroll story replaces the SaaS hero with screenshot, logo cloud and feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: The app's Bitmap Bone v2 system in a medieval pixel world: bone sky, layered hills, the red-roofed castle, a side view of the wall with a gate tunnel and portcullis, Veil's guard on the battlements, a carrier pigeon, a neutral stone tower for "AI provider" with no logo. Same fonts, frames and buttons as the app.

STORY: Hero states the promise with "Protect a document" and "Try a sample". A pinned stage tells four steps as the visitor scrolls: the document inside the walls; the portcullis rises and each value is blacked out as the document passes through the gate; the pigeon carries only placeholders to the AI provider; the answer comes home with real names. Then an interactive fix-a-miss demonstration (illustrative only; app corrections are not automatically submitted for training), the verified numbers with sources, and a final CTA panel.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky top bar with section links and "Protect a document"; centred headline, one sentence, primary and secondary CTA; below, the castle on parallax hills with flickering torches and pennants, and a "Follow a document" cue.

FORM: Bitmap Bone extended to a medieval scene; seed key 34f26c03. Signature interaction: the scroll-linked gate scene (Motion springs, reversible on scroll up; on narrow screens a camera pans along the action; reduced motion shows the finished scene and the four steps as a list).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
